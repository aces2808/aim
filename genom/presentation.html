Problem Statement

Key Challenges & Problems

1. Scaling BHPS 10x

  • Quality dilution, SLA failures, rising complaints
  • Execution complexity across dealers, associates, contractors

2. Dealer Economics

  • Margin pressure
  • Over-reliance on high-performing dealers

3. Manpower & Churn

  • Associate churn > 50%
  • Threatens MoT discipline & training scalability

4. Contractor Network

  • Rapid onboarding → inconsistent workmanship
  • TCS vs BHPS cannibalization

5. Tech & Process

  • Salesforce adoption gaps
  • Data quality & compliance risks

6. Lead Conversion

  • BHPS conversion: 6–7% vs TCS: 20–25%
  • High CAC threatens profitability
7. Operational Risks — Supply chain delays, seasonality, and "On Time or Money Back" payout exposure.
Strategic Options

Options to Solve the Problem

Option 1

Strengthen IT Controls

  • Enforce MoTs digitally
  • Automate checks, escalations, SLA tracking
  • Integrate sales, logistics, operations
Option 2

Process Re-engineering

  • Standardize customer journey
  • Simplify handoffs
  • Build regional buffer stocks
Option 3

Do Nothing (Status Quo)

  • Continue scaling current model
  • Avoid upfront investment
  • Accept rising complaints & payout risks
Option 4

Capture Info at Source

  • Require MoTs recorded on-site
  • Improve accuracy & reduce rework
Option 5

Centralized Digital Platform

  • Treat dealers, associates, contractors as one network
  • Improve SLA visibility & escalation speed
Option 6

Supply Chain Integration

  • Link inventory, scheduling, certification
  • Protect consistency during 10x scale-up
Recommendation

Hybrid Strategy — Strengthen Backbone First, Scale Second

1. Enforce MoT Compliance

  • Make MoTs non-negotiable in digital workflows
  • Reduce rework, complaints, payout exposure

2. Redesign Dealer Incentives

  • Tiered commissions + performance bonuses
  • Protect dealer economics while scaling

3. Scale Frontline Capability

  • Expand Starmaker coach network
  • Introduce partial on-roll roles to reduce churn

4. Build Capacity Buffers

  • Regional warehouses
  • Mechanized kits
  • Contractor certification ladder

5. Segment BHPS vs TCS

  • BHPS = premium, supervised
  • TCS = mass-market, trained contractor
  • Prevent cannibalization

6. Phase the Scale-Up

  • Strengthen operating model → then accelerate
  • Protect brand equity & premium product adoption
Conclusion

Final Takeaway

BHPS is strategically attractive, but scaling 10x requires:

  • Stronger process discipline
  • Digital enforcement
  • Dealer alignment
  • Frontline capability building
  • Clear segmentation
A phased, hybrid strategy ensures growth without compromising service quality.
